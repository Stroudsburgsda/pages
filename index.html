Community Services Thanksgiving Drive

Our Community Services Department is launching this year’s Thanksgiving drive. Many local families depend on the support provided by our community service center, and as Thanksgiving approaches, we want to bless them with supplies for a holiday meal.

To contribute financially, please mark "Thanksgiving Drive" on your tithe envelope before placing it in the offering collection. For all other donations, please contact Sister Nadine Gayle, Community Services Director.
