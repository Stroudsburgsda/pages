OneVoice27: Journey Through The Desire of Ages

The Pennsylvania Conference is excited to join OneVoice27, a worldwide Seventh-day Adventist Church initiative inviting members to know Jesus more deeply and share His love ahead of a global digital harvest in September 2027. Members are invited to journey through The Desire of Ages and the Gospels using a special reading journal with daily Scripture readings. Pick up your free gift journal from the pastor!
